## Executive Summary

VideoAsk, a Typeform product, transforms static lead forms and support tickets into interactive, face-to-face video conversations. It uses a branching logic engine to route users through custom paths based on their video, audio, or text responses.

### Who is VideoAsk Best For?
**Target Audience:** B2B sales teams, high-volume recruiters, and customer success managers who need to qualify leads at scale without losing the human touch.

## Key Features & Capabilities

- **Conditional Logic Branching**
- **Multi-format Responses (Video, Audio, Text, Multiple Choice)**
- **Live Video Call Escalation**
- **Speech-to-Text Transcription**
- **Contact Management & CRM Syncing**

## Detailed Verdict & Technical Analysis

VideoAsk is a specialized powerhouse for businesses that rely on trust-based selling or high-touch candidate screening. Unlike Loom, which is for internal documentation, VideoAsk is an external-facing conversion engine. Its technical superiority lies in its branching logic; you can build a 10-step automated interview or sales qualification flow that feels like a live Zoom call to the end-user. This significantly reduces lead friction compared to traditional text-based forms.

However, the tool's ROI is heavily dependent on volume. Because VideoAsk charges by the minute of processed video, B2C companies with massive traffic may find the 'Brand' plan's 1000-minute limit restrictive and expensive. For B2B organizations where a single qualified lead is worth thousands of dollars, the cost is negligible compared to the conversion lift.

We recommend VideoAsk for teams that have already optimized their top-of-funnel traffic and now need to improve lead quality and 'speed-to-lead' metrics. It is not a tool for mass-market cold outreach, but rather a sophisticated filter for mid-funnel engagement. If you don't need branching logic, a simpler tool like Bonjoro might be more cost-effective.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does VideoAsk work with my CRM?

Yes, VideoAsk has native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zapier, allowing you to map video responses and transcripts directly to lead records.

### What happens if I go over my monthly minute limit?

On paid plans, you can purchase additional minutes or upgrade your tier. On the Free plan, your VideoAsks will stop accepting responses once the 20-minute limit is reached.

### Can I use VideoAsk for live calls?

Yes, the 'Grow' and 'Brand' plans include a Live Call feature that allows users to request a real-time video session if an agent is available.

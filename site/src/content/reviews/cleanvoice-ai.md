## Detailed Verdict & Technical Analysis

Cleanvoice AI fills a hyper-specific gap in audio post-production by mastering automated surgical vocal cleanup without forcing creators to abandon their preferred Digital Audio Workstation (DAW). Unlike broad video editors that lock users into closed ecosystems, Cleanvoice operates as an efficient pre-processing layer. Its ability to extract non-lexical sounds, mouth clicks, stutters, and prolonged silences cuts dialogue cleanup time by up to 70%. Teams can export non-destructive timeline markers directly into Adobe Audition, Pro Tools, or Premiere Pro, keeping raw audio intact while bypassing hours of manual scrubbing. Audio production leads will appreciate the consistency it brings across multi-speaker panel shows. However, high-volume production studios should calculate their usage carefully. Because pricing scales on hours processed rather than flat unlimited seats, teams pushing hundreds of hours of raw audio monthly may encounter higher recurring operational costs compared to flat-fee editing software. Cleanvoice is also not designed to replace comprehensive audio mixing suite features like dynamic compression, multi-band equalization, or final LUFS mastering. For creators whose major bottleneck is listening to raw audio at double speed just to snip out filler words, stutters, and awkward pauses, Cleanvoice AI provides immediate operational ROI. Its multilingual accuracy and non-destructive DAW integration make it a specialized force multiplier for modern media teams.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Cleanvoice AI support video file uploads?

Yes, Cleanvoice allows you to upload video formats like MP4 and MOV. It extracts the underlying audio for cleanup and allows you to export marker cuts back into your NLE.

### How accurate is Cleanvoice for non-English audio?

Cleanvoice uses acoustic models specifically trained on multiple languages, achieving over 90% accuracy for filler words in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, and more.

### Can I review cuts before final rendering?

Yes, Cleanvoice provides an interactive web editor where you can listen to and override individual cuts before exporting audio or timeline markers.

### Will Cleanvoice affect the natural pacing of my podcast?

You can adjust the silence length thresholds and filler sensitivity sliders to maintain a natural conversational flow without making the audio sound clipped.

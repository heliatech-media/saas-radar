### Who is Castmagic Best For?
**Target Audience:** Podcasters, content agencies, and digital marketers producing long-form audio or video content weekly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How accurate is Castmagic's transcription engine?

Castmagic utilizes advanced speech-to-text models that handle multi-speaker conversations, industry jargon, and accents with high accuracy, though slight editing is always recommended for absolute perfection.

### Can I upload YouTube videos directly into Castmagic?

Yes, you can paste public YouTube URLs directly into the platform to ingest, transcribe, and repurpose video content without needing to download and upload large media files manually.

### How do custom prompts work on Castmagic?

Custom prompts allow you to instruct the AI to analyze your transcript using specific guidelines, enabling you to generate proprietary content styles, unique newsletter formats, or tailored social media hooks unique to your brand.

### Is there a free trial available?

Castmagic periodically offers limited trial credits or demo access for new users to test out the transcription and prompt generation engine before committing to a monthly subscription.

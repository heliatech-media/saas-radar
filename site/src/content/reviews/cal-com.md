## Executive Summary

Cal.com is a developer-first, highly customizable open-source scheduling infrastructure designed to replace legacy tools like Calendly. It offers deep API access, white-labeling capabilities, and robust routing forms that integrate directly into existing tech stacks.

### Who is Cal.com Best For?
**Target Audience:** Engineering-led teams, agencies, and enterprises requiring white-labeled, API-driven scheduling workflows.

## Key Features & Capabilities

- **Open-source codebase with self-hosting options**
- **Advanced conditional routing forms to filter and direct leads**
- **Granular team scheduling (round-robin, collective, managed events)**
- **Extensive webhook and API ecosystem for custom integrations**
- **White-label branding and custom domain mapping**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I self-host Cal.com for free?

Yes, Cal.com is open-source under the AGPLv3 license, meaning you can deploy and self-host the core application on your own infrastructure entirely free of licensing fees.

### How does Cal.com compare to Calendly?

While Calendly is a closed, consumer-friendly proprietary tool, Cal.com is developer-first, open-source, highly customizable, and offers significantly deeper API access and white-labeling.

### Does Cal.com support payment collection during booking?

Yes, Cal.com integrates directly with Stripe, allowing you to charge for consultations, coaching sessions, or services at the exact moment of scheduling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Semrush better than Ahrefs for keyword research?

Semrush generally offers a broader suite of integrated digital marketing tools including PPC and social media data, whereas Ahrefs is often praised for its ultra-fast and precise backlink index. Both have massive keyword databases, but Semrush provides more diverse marketing workflows out of the box.

### Can I use Semrush for free?

Yes, Semrush offers a free tier with strict daily limits on report generation and domain searches, which is suitable for basic keyword lookups but inadequate for ongoing SEO campaigns.

### Is Semrush suitable for local SEO?

Yes, Semrush includes robust local SEO toolkits such as listing management, position tracking by zip code/city, and local rank tracking integrations.

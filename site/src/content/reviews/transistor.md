## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I host multiple podcasts on Transistor?

Yes. Transistor allows you to manage multiple shows from a single account. Depending on your pricing tier, you can host anywhere from 2 to 10 shows (or more on custom enterprise plans) without paying separate subscription fees for each podcast.

### Does Transistor support private podcasting?

Yes, Transistor is an industry leader in private podcast distribution. You can generate secure, subscriber-specific RSS feeds for internal company training, member-only content, or paid communities.

### How do my episodes get distributed to Spotify and Apple Podcasts?

Transistor generates an RSS feed for your show. You submit this feed to major directories like Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Amazon Music just once; any future episodes you publish on Transistor will automatically sync to those platforms.

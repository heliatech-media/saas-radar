## Executive Summary

Tally is a revolutionary form builder that lets users create complex forms as easily as typing a document in Notion. By adopting a block-based, Notion-style UI, it strips away the traditional form-building complexity while keeping advanced features like conditional logic, payment collection, and custom domains accessible on its generous free tier.

### Who is Tally Best For?
**Target Audience:** Founders, indie makers, operations teams, and marketers looking for powerful forms without bloated enterprise pricing.

## Key Features & Capabilities

- **Notion-style markdown command menu for rapid form creation**
- **Advanced conditional logic and branching forms**
- **Hidden fields, URL parameters, and pre-filling capabilities**
- **Stripe integration for seamless product sales and subscriptions**
- **Calculations and formula fields for dynamic scoring and quotes**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Tally completely free?

Yes, Tally offers a remarkably generous free tier that includes unlimited forms, unlimited responses, file uploads, conditional logic, and basic integrations. You only pay if you want to remove Tally branding, use custom domains, or enable team collaboration via Tally Pro.

### Can I accept payments using Tally?

Yes, Tally integrates directly with Stripe. You can sell products, accept donations, or set up recurring subscriptions straight from your form without paying any platform transaction fees beyond standard Stripe processing rates.

### How does Tally compare to Typeform?

While Typeform is known for its conversational one-question-at-a-time design, Tally uses a Notion-style document editor. Tally is significantly cheaper, offers advanced logic and calculations on its free tier, and allows full page layouts, whereas Typeform charges heavily for response volume.

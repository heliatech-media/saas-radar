## 3. Hands-on Workshop (15-Minute Blueprint)

Ready to build your first automated lead generation pipeline? Follow this battle-tested blueprint to extract and enrich LinkedIn search results directly into your workspace.

### Step 1: Select Your Phantom
Navigate to your PhantomBuster dashboard and browse the library. Choose the **LinkedIn Search Export** Phantom. This specialized scraper is engineered to parse profile URLs, names, titles, and company data from any standard LinkedIn search or Sales Navigator query.

### Step 2: Configure Your Target Input
Paste your target LinkedIn search URL into the Phantom setup configuration. Specify the maximum number of profiles you wish to extract per launch to stay within safe daily limits. Connect your personal LinkedIn session cookie securely using the browser extension helper.

### Step 3: Set Execution Frequency & Automations
Configure your launch settings to run repeatedly—for example, "Once per workday." Next, link this Phantom to an email discovery Phantom to automatically append verified professional email addresses to your extracted list.

### Step 4: Sync with Your CRM via Webhook
Connect your finished workflow to your CRM or Google Sheets via native webhooks. Review your logs, hit launch, and watch as clean, qualified B2B leads flow straight into your sales pipeline without manual intervention.

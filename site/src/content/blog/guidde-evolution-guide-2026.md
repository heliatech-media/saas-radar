## 3. Hands-on Workshop: The 15-Minute Blueprint

You can revolutionize your internal onboarding or external support today by following this high-velocity blueprint.

### Step 1: The Zero-Friction Capture
Install the Guidde browser extension. Navigate to the workflow you want to document (e.g., setting up a new campaign in your CRM). Hit 'Capture.' Perform the task naturally. Do not worry about mistakes; the AI focuses on the successful path.

### Step 2: The AI Refinement
Once you stop the capture, Guidde will present a storyboard. Review the AI-generated text descriptions for each step. If the AI labeled a button 'Submit,' but you want it to say 'Finalize Order,' simply click and edit the text. The video syncs automatically.

### Step 3: Voiceover & Branding
Select a voice profile that matches your brand persona. Choose your language. Add your company logo and colors using the brand kit settings. Notice how the AI adjusts the timing of the video to match the length of the voiceover—no manual trimming required.

### Step 4: Multi-Channel Deployment
Don't just send a link. Use the 'Smart Embed' feature to drop the guide directly into your Notion workspace, Zendesk help center, or Slack channel. When the software updates, you can edit a single step in Guidde, and the embed updates everywhere instantly.

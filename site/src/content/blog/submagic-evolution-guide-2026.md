## 3. Hands-on Workshop (15-Minute Blueprint)

Let’s move from theory to execution. Here is your end-to-end operational blueprint for producing a high-converting short-form clip using Submagic in under 15 minutes.

### Phase 1: Ingestion & Upload
1. Navigate to your dashboard via the [official portal](/go/submagic).
2. Click **Create Video** and drag-and-drop your raw horizontal or vertical footage (MP4, MOV up to standard resolution limits).
3. Select your primary spoken language from the dropdown menu to optimize the speech-to-text accuracy model.

### Phase 2: Template Selection & AI Styling
1. Once transcription completes, head over to the **Captions** tab.
2. Choose a pre-built style template (e.g., the Hormozi-style bold yellow-and-white preset for high-impact authority branding).
3. Review the auto-highlighted keywords. Toggle any words you want emphasized or corrected directly in the inline text editor.

### Phase 3: Visual Polish & B-Roll Integration
1. Navigate to the **B-Roll / Magic** panel.
2. Enable **Auto B-Roll** and set the density level to moderate to prevent visual overload.
3. Turn on **Smart Zooms** to let the AI automatically scale and re-frame the video on significant audio beats.

### Phase 4: Export & Distribution
1. Preview the final render in the web viewport to ensure lip-sync and caption cadence feel natural.
2. Hit **Export**, select your preferred resolution (1080p vertical for Reels/TikTok/Shorts), and download your file or push it directly into your social scheduling queue.

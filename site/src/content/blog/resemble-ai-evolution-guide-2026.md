The friction was palpable: updating a product video meant re-hiring talent, re-booking studios, and waiting weeks for post-production. Resemble AI didn't just iterate on this; they dismantled the legacy model entirely.

## 3. Hands-on Workshop: The 15-Minute Blueprint

1.  **Preparation (Minutes 0-3):** Gather 5-10 minutes of high-quality, clean audio of your target voice. Ensure there is no background noise; hiss and reverb are the enemies of high-fidelity clones.
2.  **Ingestion (Minutes 3-6):** Upload your files to the Resemble dashboard. Use the "Voice Creation" tool to map the phonemes. The platform will automatically process the audio to extract the unique timbre.
3.  **Synthesis (Minutes 6-10):** Navigate to the "Speech" editor. Input your script. Adjust the 'Prosody' sliders to tweak the pitch and inflection of critical sentences.
4.  **API Deployment (Minutes 10-15):** Once satisfied with the quality, navigate to the API settings. Generate your API key and integrate the WebSocket endpoint into your staging environment to test the latency in your live application.

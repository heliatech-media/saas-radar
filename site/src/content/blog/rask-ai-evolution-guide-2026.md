## 3. Hands-on Workshop: The 15-Minute Global Blueprint

Ready to scale? Follow this executive workflow to take a local asset global by lunch.

1.  **Asset Ingestion:** Upload your master video file (up to 3GB) to the Rask dashboard. For best results, ensure the audio is clean and free of heavy background music.
2.  **Language Selection & Analysis:** Select your target languages from the list of 130+. Rask will begin its 'Diarization' process, identifying every speaker in the video.
3.  **Voice Assignment:** Choose to either 'Clone Original' (highly recommended for brand consistency) or select from Rask’s library of professional voice skins if the original audio is poor.
4.  **The Polish Phase:** Review the generated transcript. Rask allows you to edit the text directly; the AI will automatically regenerate the audio to match your edits in real-time.
5.  **Visual Alignment:** Trigger the 'Lip-Sync' feature for your top-performing snippets to ensure the visual experience matches the auditory one.
6.  **Export & Distribute:** Download your localized files in 4K resolution and use the 'Shorts' tool to create a week's worth of social content in one click.

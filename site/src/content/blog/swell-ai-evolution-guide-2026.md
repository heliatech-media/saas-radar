## 3. Hands-on Workshop: The 15-Minute Automated Editorial Blueprint

To demonstrate how Swell AI eliminates editorial manual labor, let’s walk through setting up an end-to-end automated post-production machine.

```
+-----------------------+     +-----------------------+     +-----------------------+
| Raw Audio / RSS Feed  | --> |   Swell AI Engine     | --> | Multi-Channel Outputs |
| (Podcast / Webinar)   |     | (Whisper + Prompts)   |     | (Show Notes, Blogs)   |
+-----------------------+     +-----------------------+     +-----------------------+
                                          |                        |
                                          v                        v
                               +---------------------+  +---------------------+ 
                               | Outbound Webhooks   |  | CMS Auto-Publishing | 
                               | (Zapier / Slack)    |  | (WordPress / HubSpot)| 
                               +---------------------+  +---------------------+ 
```

### Step 1: Connect Your Show’s RSS Feed
1. Log into your Swell AI dashboard and navigate to **Integrations**.
2. Click **Add Podcast RSS Feed** and paste your public feed URL (e.g., Apple Podcasts or Spotify RSS).
3. Toggle on **Auto-Process New Episodes**. Swell AI will now passively monitor your feed and begin processing the second a new audio file is published.

### Step 2: Customize Your Brand Voice & Prompts
1. Select **Prompt Templates** from the sidebar menu.
2. Edit the **Show Notes** template to enforce your brand’s specific structural guidelines (e.g., *"Always start with a two-sentence executive summary, followed by five bullet points with precise timestamps in [HH:MM:SS] format"*).
3. Adjust the **SEO Blog** template to specify targeted keyword density, target reading level, and required CTA footers.

### Step 3: Configure Outbound CMS Integrations
1. Navigate to **Export Integrations** and connect your target platform (e.g., WordPress or [Webflow](/reviews/webflow)).
2. Set default publishing states to **Draft**. This ensures AI-generated articles are staged inside your editorial queue for human approval rather than instantly pushed live.

### Step 4: Execute the Human-in-the-Loop Review Pipeline
1. Once an episode processes, review the generated transcript in the Swell AI editor.
2. Verify speaker tags and make any quick corrections using the global search-and-replace tool.
3. Click **Export All** to push show notes, social threads, and blog drafts straight into your content stack, saving hours per episode.

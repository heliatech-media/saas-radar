## 1. The Genesis & Evolution: Disrupting Legacy Dinosaurs

Before Lemlist entered the market, cold email software was trapped in the dark ages of plain text and reckless mass-blasting. Sales teams were flying blind, destroying their sender reputations, and accepting a standard 1% response rate as an unchangeable law of physics.

The spark behind Lemlist was radical transparency and innovation: What if cold emails could look like they were individually crafted by hand, complete with custom images showing the prospect's website, logo, or name directly inside the visual creative? What if domain deliverability could be actively managed and protected by a peer-to-peer warm-up network?

By challenging legacy dinosaurs that cared more about raw volume than inbox placement, Lemlist rapidly grew from an underdog tool into an enterprise-grade powerhouse. It forced the entire B2B marketing industry to raise its standards, shifting the focus from quantity to quality, relevance, and relentless deliverability protection.

## 3. Hands-on Workshop (15-Minute Blueprint)

Ready to launch your first high-converting campaign? Follow this step-by-step masterclass blueprint to build an elite outbound sequence in under 15 minutes.

### Step 1: Connect and Warm Up Your Sending Domain
Navigate to the settings dashboard, connect your custom Google Workspace or Outlook SMTP/IMAP credentials, and activate **lemwarm**. Let your domain run through the warm-up pool for at least 7 to 14 days before ramping up high-volume sends.

### Step 2: Build and Verify Your Lead List
Use the built-in Lead Finder or upload a targeted CSV file containing first names, last names, company names, titles, and verified email addresses. Clean your data to ensure zero syntax errors or role-based addresses (like info@ or support@).

### Step 3: Craft Your Dynamic Creative Template
Draft a concise, pain-point-driven email. Instead of a generic text block, insert a Lemlist custom image block. Overlay the prospect's website screenshot onto a mockup device. Set fallback text to ensure the email still makes sense if the image fails to load.

### Step 4: Design the Multi-Channel Sequence
Set up your automated flow:
- **Day 1:** Initial email with dynamic image.
- **Day 3:** LinkedIn profile view and polite follow-up checking if they saw your creative.
- **Day 7:** Short text-only "bump" email addressing a specific secondary pain point.
- **Day 10:** Automated cold call task assigned directly to your SDR.

### Step 5: Launch and A/B Test
Split-test two different subject lines and two distinct custom image thumbnails. Monitor the real-time analytics dashboard to track open rates, click rates, and reply rates, then optimize your messaging mid-flight.

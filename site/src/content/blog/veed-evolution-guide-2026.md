## 1. The Genesis & Evolution

Founded in 2018 by Sabba Keynejad and Tim Seed, VEED was born out of a frustrating personal realization: editing videos online was painfully difficult, fragmented, and slow. While legacy giants like Adobe Premiere Pro and Apple Final Cut Pro catered to career editors with deep hardware rigs, everyday creators, social media managers, and digital marketers were left stranded. They had to stitch together makeshift toolsets—one app for subtitles, another for compression, a third for resizing, and separate transcription software.

Keynejad and Seed envisioned a radical alternative: an all-in-one, browser-based video suite that required zero installation, ran smoothly on modest hardware, and leveraged modern web technologies to democratize high-end post-production. As generative AI exploded onto the SaaS landscape, VEED integrated intelligent automation directly into its core timeline. Today, it stands as a category-defining AI video platform, disrupting legacy software dinosaurs by empowering non-technical marketing teams to produce broadcast-quality, localized, and hyper-engaging video assets at unprecedented velocity.

## 3. Hands-on Workshop (15-Minute Blueprint)

Let’s put VEED to the test. Imagine you need to transform a 10-minute executive webinar recording into three high-impact, captioned social clips optimized for LinkedIn, complete with brand styling and localized subtitles, in under 15 minutes.

### Step 1: Ingestion & Workspace Setup
Navigate to VEED via our [exclusive partnership link](/go/veed) and create your workspace. Drag and drop your raw 10-minute webinar MP4 into the browser timeline. VEED will instantly parse the file and prompt you to select your target canvas format (choose 9:16 for vertical shorts or 1:1 for square LinkedIn feeds).

### Step 2: AI Subtitle Generation & Styling
1. Click on the **Subtitles** tab on the left navigation menu.
2. Select **Auto Transcript** and choose the primary spoken language. 
3. Within seconds, review the generated transcript. [Make](/reviews/make) quick inline edits if technical jargon was misspelled.
4. Apply a dynamic animation style (such as karaoke-highlight or pop-up captioning) from the style selector, matching your brand’s aesthetic guidelines.

### Step 3: Cleanup & AI Enhancement
1. Click on the audio track on your timeline and toggle **Clean Audio** to eliminate background room hum and static.
2. If the speaker's eyes occasionally drifted away from the camera lens, enable **Eye Contact Correction** under the AI tools menu to maintain continuous viewer engagement.

### Step 4: Export & Distribution
Hit the **Export** button in the top right corner. Select your desired resolution (1080p or 4K), remove the watermark via your active tier, and either download the rendered MP4 or copy the direct collaboration review link to share with stakeholders instantly.

## 1. The Genesis & Evolution

Founded by industry veterans Keith Peiris and Henri Lemoine, Tome emerged from a shared frustration with the static, uninspired formats dominating modern business communications. Observing that traditional presentation tools were essentially digital facsimiles of physical slide projectors from the mid-20th century, the founders asked a disruptive question: What if a presentation was built like an interactive, responsive website rather than a static slide deck?

By harnessing advanced large language models and native image generation capabilities, Tome broke away from the legacy dinosaur software monopoly. It didn't just add an AI assistant to an existing toolbar; it completely re-architected the medium. Instead of forcing users to manually draw shapes and align text boxes, Tome introduced an interactive canvas where intent translates immediately into structured, beautifully formatted storytelling. It quickly became the go-to utility for fast-moving startups and enterprise innovation teams looking to compress weeks of design work into a single afternoon.

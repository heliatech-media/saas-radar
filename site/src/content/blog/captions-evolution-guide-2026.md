Before the advent of generative AI video suites, a marketing team wishing to publish a single polished talking-head clip had to marshal an army of specialists: a scriptwriter, a teleprompter wrangler, an audio engineer to scrub room echo, a transcriptionist to burn custom kinetic subtitles, and a localization agency to dub the footage into foreign markets. The human bottleneck was staggering, stretching a 60-second social clip across a multi-day post-production pipeline. Today, that legacy workflow belongs in a museum.

## 3. Hands-on Workshop (15-Minute Blueprint)

Ready to transform your video workflow? Follow this step-by-step masterclass blueprint to produce, polish, and translate a professional talking-head video in under fifteen minutes.

### Step 1: Script & Record with the Built-In Teleprompter
Open the Captions app on your mobile device or desktop. Paste your marketing script into the integrated teleprompter. Adjust the scroll speed to match your natural cadence, position your eyes near the camera lens, and hit record. Trust the software to capture clean video while you maintain flawless delivery.

### Step 2: Auto-Generate and Style Kinetic Subtitles
Import your raw clip into the editor. Navigate to the captions tab and tap generate. Within seconds, your transcript appears on screen. Choose from high-converting dynamic templates, tweaking font weights, highlight colors, and kinetic animation speeds to match your brand guidelines.

### Step 3: Clean Audio and Apply Eye-Contact Correction
 Tap the enhancement suite. Toggle on background noise removal to strip out environmental interference. Next, enable the AI eye-contact correction filter to ensure your gaze remains locked directly on the viewer, creating an intimate, high-trust connection regardless of where your eyes wandered during recording.

### Step 4: Localize and Dub for Global Reach
Select the 'Dubbing' feature, pick your target language (e.g., Spanish, German, or Japanese), and let the AI synthesize a localized voice track complete with automated lip-syncing. Preview the output, export in 4K resolution, and deploy across your global distribution channels.

### 1. Global Contractor Management: ~$25 – $30 / Contractor / Month
*   **Target Profile:** Distributed freelancers, independent consultants, and B2B service providers operating across jurisdictions where direct entities are unnecessary.
*   **Cost Mechanics:** Billed monthly per active contractor. Includes automated local-law compliant contract generation, tax ID collection (W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E / local equivalents), and automated milestone or time-based invoicing.
*   **Volume Variance:** Enterprises managing upwards of 250+ contractors routinely negotiate volume-tiered reductions approaching the $18–$22 band, though this triggers minimum utilization clauses in the master contract.

### 2. Full Employer of Record (EOR): ~$540 – $790 / Employee / Month
*   **Target Profile:** International hires in jurisdictions where the enterprise lacks a registered legal entity, requiring Papaya to act as the legal local employer.
*   **Cost Mechanics:** This is a fixed management fee per head, completely decoupled from the employee's gross salary. 
*   **Geographic Variance:** Low-risk, high-infrastructure jurisdictions (e.g., United Kingdom, Western Europe) trend toward the lower bound ($540–$620). High-friction jurisdictions with complex statutory termination laws, mandatory severance reserves, and strict labor unions (e.g., Brazil, France, India) command the upper bound ($700–$790+).

### 3. Unified Global Payroll (Client Entities): ~$3 – $12 / Employee / Month
*   **Target Profile:** Multinational enterprises that already maintain local legal entities in various countries but require a unified consolidation layer for payroll execution, general ledger (GL) integration, and statutory remittance.
*   **Cost Mechanics:** Base platform fees scale inversely with headcount. While small deployments (under 50 employees) sit near the $12 mark, global footprints exceeding 1,000 employees see base processing fees compress down to $3–$5 per employee per month. Note that this excludes local in-country partner (ICP) fees where Papaya orchestrates rather than directly executes.

## Hidden Surcharges Decoded: What Sales Reps Don't Lead With

A procurement audit is incomplete without evaluating the marginal transactional costs embedded beneath the headline rate card. Enterprise finance teams frequently encounter budget variance due to three primary non-software charges:

```
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| Surcharge Vector                  | Typical Financial Impact          | Mitigation / Enterprise Strategy      |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| Foreign Exchange (FX) Spread      | 0.5% – 3.2% over mid-market rate  | Enforce multi-currency treasury accounts |
| Off-Cycle Payroll Runs            | $100 – $250 per execution         | Consolidate ad-hoc bonuses into primary cycles |
| Statutory Severance Escrow        | 1x – 3x monthly gross salary      | Negotiate Letter of Credit (LC) alternatives |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
```

### Foreign Exchange (FX) Banking Spreads
When funding global payroll or EOR headcounts from a centralized corporate treasury (e.g., USD or EUR), funds must be converted into local fiat currencies (e.g., JPY, MXN, ZAR). 
*   **The Trap:** Standard providers route conversions through legacy correspondent banking networks, embedding a 1.5% to 3.5% markup into the daily spot rate. On a $10M annual payroll volume, this hidden friction bleeds up to $350,000 silently.
*   **The Papaya Countermeasure:** Papaya utilizes licensed Tier-1 banking partners and direct multi-currency wallets. Enterprise accounts can negotiate transparent FX markups pegged directly to institutional spot rates (often sub-40 basis points), provided funding is pre-deposited into dedicated virtual IBANs.

### Off-Cycle Payroll & Correction Runs
Mistakes happen—missed commission calculations, unrecorded expense reimbursements, or urgent onboarding adjustments. 
*   **The Trap:** Standard SLAs cover one primary monthly payroll cycle per jurisdiction. Requesting an "off-cycle" or emergency run triggers manual statutory recalculations and bank wire fees. Vendors typically bill $100 to $250 per employee for these interventions.
*   **The Procurement Fix:** Mandate an allotment of *N* complimentary off-cycle runs per quarter within the Master Services Agreement to hedge against human error during rapid scaling phases.

### Local Statutory Severance Deposit Escrow Mechanics
In many global jurisdictions (particularly across Latin America and parts of EMEA), labor laws mandate that employers accrue and escrow statutory termination indemnities monthly.
*   **The Trap:** EOR providers often require upfront cash deposits equal to 1 to 3 months of gross salary per employee held in non-interest-bearing escrow accounts to mitigate abandonment risk. For a 50-person EOR deployment in high-risk zones, this ties up hundreds of thousands in working capital.
*   **The Procurement Fix:** Insist on replacing cash escrow requirements with a corporate parent guarantee or a standby Letter of Credit (LC), preserving your internal liquidity for core R&D and growth initiatives.

## Executive Strategy: How to Negotiate Enterprise Terms

Entering a sales dialogue with global payroll vendors unprepared exposes your finance team to inflated rate cards. Leverage these four tactical strategies during your procurement cycle:

1.  **Demand Rate Card Locking for Headcount Spikes:** Never accept linear pricing scaling. Insist on a volume step-down matrix in your MSA that automatically triggers fee reductions when your EOR or contractor headcount crosses predefined thresholds (e.g., 25, 50, 100, 250 heads).
2.  **Audit the Implementation Fee:** Enterprise sales teams often pad implementation and data-migration fees as a negotiable discount lever. Push for complete waiver of onboarding costs in exchange for signing a multi-year (24 to 36 month) commitment.
3.  **Establish Clear SLA Penalties for Statutory Remittances:** Global payroll failure is not merely an inconvenience; it triggers severe tax penalties from local governments. Ensure your contract includes indemnification clauses where the vendor assumes full financial responsibility for penalties resulting from calculation or remittance errors on their end.
4.  **Leverage Authorized Channel Partners:** Bypassing standard inbound sales forms and utilizing pre-negotiated enterprise evaluation channels can unlock prioritized onboarding queues, waived platform implementation fees, and extended sandbox testing environments.

## 3. Hands-on Workshop (15-Minute Blueprint)

Let’s translate theory into immediate operational velocity. Here is how you can set up a high-converting automated lead qualification and routing workflow using Zapier in less than fifteen minutes.

### Step 1: Establish Your Trigger Event
Navigate to your Zapier dashboard and click "Create Zap." Select your intake tool (e.g., Typeform or Google Forms) as your Trigger app, and choose "New Entry" or "New Response." Connect your account and run a test to pull in live sample data.

### Step 2: Implement Data Enrichment or Filtering
Add an Action step. To ensure only high-intent leads progress, insert a "Filter by Zapier" step. Set the rule: *Continue only if Company Size greater than 50*. Alternatively, integrate an AI step to instantly summarize the lead's open-text submission for your sales reps.

### Step 3: Route to Your CRM and Slack
Add a subsequent Action step linking to your core CRM (such as HubSpot or Salesforce) to automatically create or update the contact record. Finally, add a multi-app action or parallel path to dispatch a high-priority alert directly into a dedicated Slack channel for your closing team.

### Step 4: Test, Review, and Turn Live
Execute a comprehensive end-to-end test. Once Zapier confirms a successful data payload transmission across all integrated apps, toggle your workflow from draft to live. You have just automated a sequence that previously required manual oversight.

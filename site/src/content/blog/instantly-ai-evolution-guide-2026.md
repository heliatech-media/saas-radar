## 3. Hands-on Workshop: 15-Minute Blueprint

Ready to scale? Follow this technical sequence to launch your first high-reputation campaign.

1.  **Domain Acquisition:** Purchase 5-10 domains specifically for outreach (e.g., get-company.com instead of company.com).
2.  **DNS Hardening:** Within your domain registrar, configure your SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records. This is non-negotiable for deliverability.
3.  **Account Integration:** Connect your new email accounts to Instantly.ai. 
4.  **The Warmup Phase:** Toggle the 'Warmup' feature. Let these accounts 'age' for 14 days before sending your first cold campaign.
5.  **Campaign Launch:** Use the built-in lead filter to segment your audience, upload your CSV, and set your daily sending limits to 30-50 emails per account to start.
